# Privacy Policy

**SushiStack** respects your privacy.  

- **No Personal Data Collected**  
  This app does not collect, store, or share any personal information.  

- **Local Usage Only**  
  All data (your counted plates) stays on your device and is never transmitted to us or third parties.  

- **No Third-Party Services**  
  SushiStack does not use analytics, ads, or tracking tools.  

- **Children’s Privacy**  
  The app is safe for all ages, as no personal data is collected.  

- **Changes to This Policy**  
  If updates are made to this Privacy Policy, the new version will be published here.
